[SIZE=4][B]RafflePlus[/B] allows you to create engaging lottery events on your server. Players can purchase tickets using in-game currency, and the system automatically selects winners based on your configuration. It's the perfect way to sink economy money and keep players excited!

What makes [B]RafflePlus[/B] special is its simplicity and visual appeal. With an intuitive GUI, players can easily understand how to participate without complex commands.[/SIZE]

[CENTER][SIZE=6][B]PLUGIN PROPERTIES[/B][/SIZE][/CENTER]

[LIST]
[SIZE=4]
[*] [B]Ticket System:[/B]
   - Configurable price per ticket.
   - Limit tickets per player (Optional).
[*] [B]Rewards:[/B]
   - Execute custom commands for winners.
   - Give items or money directly.
[*] [B]Automation:[/B]
   - Set automatic intervals for raffles.
   - Broadcast messages to announce winners.
[*] [B]Customization:[/B]
   - Edit all lore and item names in the GUI.
   - PlaceholderAPI support (Coming soon).
[/SIZE]
[/LIST]

[CENTER][IMG]https://i.imgur.com/vdk0Q5C.png[/IMG][/CENTER]

[CODE]
/raffle           - Opens the main Raffle GUI.
/raffle buy       - Quick command to buy a ticket.
/raffle reload    - Reloads the configuration file.
[/CODE]

[CENTER][IMG]https://i.imgur.com/lxNkr6T.png[/IMG][/CENTER]

[LIST]
[SIZE=4]
[*] [FONT=Courier New]r.buy[/FONT] - Allows players to open the menu and buy tickets (Default: true).
[*] [FONT=Courier New]r.reload[/FONT] - Admin permission to reload the plugin (Default: OP).
[/SIZE]
[/LIST]

[SIZE=4][B]1. Download [COLOR=#FF8C00]RafflePlus.jar[/COLOR].
2. Install [COLOR=#FF8C00]Vault[/COLOR] and an economy plugin (like EssentialsX).
3. Drag RafflePlus into your [FONT=Courier New]plugins[/FONT] folder.
4. Restart your server.
5. Configure [FONT=Courier New]config.yml[/FONT] to your liking![/B][/SIZE]
